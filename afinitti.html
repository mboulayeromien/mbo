Why Choose Afinitti?

Designed Specifically for the Bilingual Secondary System

Afinitti is built from the ground up to address the unique complexities of secondary school administration. It coordinates class-subject allocations, dual-section curricula, and disciplinary records into a unified digital ledger.

Bilingual Dual-Section Support

Configured to manage both Anglophone classes (Form 1 to Upper Sixth) and Francophone classes (6ème to Terminale) in a single unified system.

Smart Digital Badges & PVC IDs

Empower students with instantly verifiable digital badges on their smartphones, or batch-print secure PVC identity cards featuring custom QR codes for scanner attendance and library access.

Health Alerts & Nurse Logs

Every visit to the school clinic triggers an email or SMS notification to the parent, recording symptoms, treatments, and student health histories.

Integrated Disciplinary Tracker

Log disciplinary infractions with demerit ratings. Helps the Discipline Master calculate automated conduct marks directly into sequence reports.

1-Click Report Cards & Transcripts

Generate breathtaking, coefficient-weighted report cards and official student transcripts in milliseconds. Averages, class ranks, and conduct remarks are computed instantly with zero manual effort, delivering error-free PDFs.

Early Warning Metrics

Smart relational database queries flag drop-out risks by cross-referencing high absent rates, low academic marks, and tuition dues.

AI Diagnostics & Analytics

Leverage advanced artificial intelligence to diagnose academic performance trends and generate actionable insights for both teachers and parents.

Campus-Wide Chats & Communication

A robust communication ecosystem featuring campus-wide chats, direct messaging, and global announcements to keep staff, students, and parents seamlessly connected.

Frictionless Student Registration

Transform the chaotic back-to-school rush into a breeze! Digitize your entire enrollment workflow with 1-click demographic capture.

Student Government & Prefects

Foster leadership and civic responsibility! Empower school prefects and student councils with dedicated management tools to coordinate campus activities, organize initiatives, and oversee student welfare.
