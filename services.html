Who Pays

Students, parents, School Admins, Lecturers, and guardians enrolled in partner educational institutions. All payers are registered users with verified matricule numbers in the database before any payment is authorized.

What They Pay For

School fees (tuition), student accommodation/hostel bookings, administrative registrations, official document downloads, and access tokens to advanced digital EdTech modules like AI diagnostics, blockchain certificates, and analytics dashboards.

How Funds Are Routed

MBO Inc. uses a database-enforced split-settlement model. Core academic fees go directly to the institution's merchant account (100%). MBO platform utility charges are retained by MBO Inc. (100%). Some fees (e.g. Transcripts) are divided 50/50.

Business Model

How Our Payment Architecture Works

Designed for transparency and automated split settlement — no manual fund routing required.

Payment Flow

1
Student / Parent initiates payment
Selects a service on the iwedu student portal. Their matricule and billing identity are pre-verified in the school database.
2
gateway provider processes the transaction
Customer selects Mobile Money (MTN MoMo, Orange Money and More) or Card (Visa, Mastercard). gateway provider securely encrypts and processes the payment.
3
iwedu receives webhook confirmation
On successful payment, gateway provider sends a webhook to our server. The database marks the invoice as Paid and records the gateway reference ID.
4
Automated digital receipt delivered
An email and/or SMS receipt is sent to the user. The portal unlocks the paid service — a downloadable PDF, a grade dashboard, or a hostel room allocation.

Fund Settlement Architecture

School Merchant Account (100%)

School Fees (Tuition), Registration Fees, Annual Medical Fees, Hostel Accommodation, Library Fines, and Application Fast-Track fees are settled 100% into the partner institution's merchant account. MBO Inc. receives zero from these categories.

MBO Inc. Platform Account (100%)

Platform Maintenance Charges, Result Processing Fees, Admission Letter downloads, Attestation Reprints, AI Diagnostics, Blockchain Certificates, Analytics, Video Conferencing access, and Campus Pay wallet operations are settled 100% into MBO Inc.'s account as technical service fees.

Shared Split (50% / 50%)

Official Transcript fees are split equally: 50% to the school (printing costs, registrar management) and 50% to MBO Inc. (digital validation, e-signature network).

Legal & Compliance

Merchant Legal Policies

Required compliance documentation for payment gateway onboarding review.

1. Parties & Scope: These Terms govern all payment transactions initiated through the MBO Inc. iwedu portal by students, parents, or authorized guardians. By completing a payment, you agree to be bound by these terms.

2. Student Verification: Each transaction must be linked to a valid student matricule number registered in our database. MBO Inc. is not liable for payments made against incorrect or unverified matricule entries by the payer.

3. Payment Processing: All monetary transactions are processed by our licensed payment gateway partner. We do not store raw card data, PINs, or CVV numbers on our servers. Tokenization is applied to all card-based transactions.

4. Settlement Routing: Fund routing follows our documented split-settlement schema. Academic fees (tuition, housing, registrations) are routed to partner school accounts. Technical platform fees (digital services, AI add-ons) are routed to MBO Inc. This model is enforced at the database level per service ID.

5. Governing Law: These Terms are governed by the laws of the Republic of Cameroon and CEMAC financial regulations. Disputes are subject to the jurisdiction of the courts of Kumba, Southwest Region.

Data Controller: Mboulaye Business Organizations (MBO Inc.), Kumba, Southwest Region, Cameroon. Email: privacy@mymbo.org

Data Collected: For payment processing we collect: full name, student matricule, billing email address, and mobile phone number. Card details are never stored on our servers — they are tokenized and handled exclusively by our payment processor.

Data Sharing: Transaction data is transmitted to our payment gateway solely for authorization and settlement clearing. We do not sell, rent, or share personal data with third parties for commercial purposes.

Customer Rights: Customers may request access to their transaction history, correction of billing errors, or account deletion by contacting privacy@mymbo.org. Requests are processed within 7 business days.

Data Retention: Financial transaction records are retained for 7 years in compliance with Cameroonian accounting regulations. Inactive student portal accounts are archived after 3 academic years.

Academic Fees (Tuition, Hostel, Registration): These are settled to the partner school institution. Refund eligibility is governed by the respective institution's internal policy and academic calendar. Refund requests must be submitted in writing to the school bursar's office. MBO Inc. will facilitate the reversal process once the school approves the refund.

Digital Services & Platform Charges: Charges for digital access tokens (admission letters, attestation reprints, AI analytics credits, video conferencing sessions, blockchain certificates) are non-refundable once the digital asset or access token has been generated and delivered to the student's account.

Double Billing / Gateway Errors: If a duplicate charge occurs due to network failure or gateway error, the customer must report it within 14 days with the transaction reference to support@mymbo.org. Verified duplicate transactions will be refunded in full to the original payment method within 3–5 business days.

Chargeback Policy: Customers are encouraged to contact our support desk before initiating a chargeback with their card provider or telecom. We aim to resolve all billing disputes within 48 hours. Fraudulent chargeback claims will be contested with full transaction evidence.
