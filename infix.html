Why Choose infix?

Built for Primary and Nursery Education

infix is tailored for the foundational years of education, providing the perfect blend of pedagogical tools for teachers and administrative oversight for school leaders.

AI Pedagogical Telemetry

Teachers can record quick voice memos on student behavior and milestones. Our AI engine instantly structures and translates observations into formal reports.

Sequential Gradebooks

Rapid-entry spreadsheet interfaces for teachers to input sequential and terminal marks, automatically generating official report cards and transcripts with student photos.

Strict Financial Control

Track tuition revenue against explicitly allocated academic year budgets. Monitor operational expenses and view real-time Profit & Loss statements.

Attendance Tracking

Track attendance of pupils and staff members daily. Generate instant attendance reports for parents and administrators.

Digital Lesson Planning

Digitize weekly schemes of work and lesson notes. Track syllabus coverage and seamlessly submit plans for administrative approval.

Discipline & Conduct Ledger

Formally log positive behaviors (Merits) and infractions. Keep parents informed and automatically sync conduct scores to end-of-term report cards.
